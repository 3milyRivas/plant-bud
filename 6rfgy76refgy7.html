🌿 Care

Essential Care

for Ornamental Plants

Keep your plants healthy and visually appealing with proper care practices 🌱

🌞

Proper Lighting

Most plants thrive in indirect sunlight. Rotate weekly for balanced growth.

💧

Responsible Watering

Avoid overwatering. Ensure proper drainage and check soil moisture.

🌱

Soil & Nutrition

Use organic soil and fertilize regularly for healthy growth.

✂️

Pruning & Cleaning

Remove dead leaves and clean foliage to improve photosynthesis.

🏡

Environment

Avoid temperature changes and keep plants in stable environments.

👁️

Observation

Detect issues early like yellow leaves or drooping stems.
